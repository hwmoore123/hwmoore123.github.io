Computers used to come from garages. People built them by hand on workbenches, out of parts, because they believed a machine on your desk could make you think bigger.

Posters used to melt. Lettering bent to fill its shape, and colors sat next to each other until the edges hummed.

This site borrows a little from both. The structure underneath is a strict grid, because I like things that work. The surface moves, slowly, because I like things that feel alive.

If it's too much, the knob in the corner goes all the way down to **Sober**. Everything still works there.
